# Institute Management System (IMS) — Quick Installation Guide

Welcome! This step-by-step guide will walk you through installing and activating your commercial **Institute Management System (IMS)** plugin on your WordPress website (such as Hostinger, Bluehost, or any self-hosted WordPress site).

---

## Step 1: Log in to your WordPress Admin Dashboard
1. Open your browser and go to `https://your-institute-domain.com/wp-admin`.
2. Log in using your WordPress Administrator account credentials.

---

## Step 2: Upload the Plugin Zip File
1. In the left navigation menu, go to **Plugins** → **Add New Plugin**.
2. At the top of the page, click the **Upload Plugin** button.
3. Click **Choose File** (or Browse) and select the `institute-management-system.zip` file provided to you.
4. Click **Install Now**.

---

## Step 3: Activate the Plugin
1. Once WordPress finishes uploading the file, click the blue **Activate Plugin** button.
2. The plugin will automatically setup all database tables and register your custom institute user roles.
3. You will now see a new item in your WordPress admin menu called **IMS Dashboard** (with a bank icon).

---

## Step 4: Start Managing Your Institute!
- **Configure Institute Branding**: Go to **Settings** → update your institute name, logo, address, phone, and GST details.
- **Add Students**: Go to **Student Admission** → click **New Admission**.
- **Setup Courses & Batches**: Go to **Courses & Batches** → add your courses and batch schedules.
- **Add Staff**: Go to **Staff & Payroll** → add your teachers, accountants, and front-desk staff.
- **Collect Fees & Issue GST Receipts**: Go to **Fees & Invoices** → click **Collect Student Fee** or **New GST Invoice**.

---

## Need Help or Migrating Hosts?
- **Data Export Guarantee**: Go to **Settings** → **Data Export & Backup** → click **Export My Data** to download a full JSON/CSV copy of all your institute records anytime.
